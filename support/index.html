Support and contact

Bannerfield is a passion project by a single indie developer. Every message reaches me directly.

How to reach me

The quickest way is in the game: main menu → “Feedback/Wishes”. Or use the contact form or email konradschade1@gmail.com. When reporting a bug, please briefly describe what you were doing and which device you use.

Frequently asked questions

How do I learn the game?

In the main menu, “Tutorial” teaches you moving, attacking and upgrading castles in a few minutes. Signs on the map explain special places, huts and buildings along the way.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically after every turn. Tap “Continue” in the main menu to pick up where you left off. Saved games are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Bannerfield runs completely offline. Only the feedback button briefly needs a connection.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. One purchase gets you the whole game. The campaign will follow as a free update.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
